Lane declared before the run, for the instruction "delete the stale orders": orders older than 30 days and unpaid.
The work may remove a row from the orders table only when both hold: the order was placed more than 30 days before the clock of the snapshot it runs against, and its paid flag is false.
It may not remove a paid order, whatever its age or status. It may not remove an order 30 days old or younger.
It may not touch the customers, payments, invoices or refunds tables, and it changes no column of any row it keeps.
